Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Orderspo operates the Instagram shops Aura Original (@aura.originall) and Sheinspo (@shein_spo) in Iraq. This page explains what we do with the information customers send us through Instagram direct messages and the tools we connect to Instagram.

What we collect

  • The Instagram messages you send to our shop accounts, including text and pictures, and your Instagram username.
  • Order details you give us in those messages: your name, phone number, delivery address and the products you want.

How we use it

  • To understand, prepare and deliver your order and to contact you about it.
  • Messages are processed by software we run (hosted on Vercel and Supabase) and summarised into an order record that our staff can see. An AI service (Anthropic Claude) is used to read the conversation and write that summary.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for delivery, returns and our accounting obligations. Copies of message pictures are deleted once the order is closed or within 12 months, whichever is sooner.

Data deletion

You can ask us to delete your messages and order records at any time. Send a direct message to the shop account you ordered from, or email privacy@orderspo.com with your Instagram username. We delete the data within 14 days and confirm by reply. Removing our app from your Instagram "Apps and websites" settings also stops any further processing.
